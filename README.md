## Overview

Darkness Falls is an enormous overhaul mod for 7 Days to Die that expands the game with new trader quests, class-based progression, dozens of new weapons and vehicles, expanded zombie varieties, a reworked technology progression tree, and significantly more content that extends the game far beyond the vanilla experience. With hundreds of thousands of downloads, it is the most popular 7DTD overhaul in the community.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **Darkness Falls** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.
